01 HOW WE DESIGN

Every project in this practice follows the same four-stage discipline — regardless of scale. This case study walks that discipline end-to-end, from raw site conditions through to a fully modelled, walkable First Floor addition.

01
SITE ANALYSIS
Plot orientation, existing structural envelope, plinth area, load paths, and entry geometry surveyed before a single line is drawn.
02

03
STRUCTURAL LOGIC
Load-bearing alignment, foundation tolerance, and zone separation resolved with the consulting engineer before drafting.
04
EXECUTION

Structural & Access
Staircase layout & structural alignment
  • Internal Staircase (SE quadrant) — clockwise ascent, family-exclusive. Lands directly into Family Suite on FF, then continues up to roof level through a stair-head enclosure with a hinged, lockable door — terrace can be secured from the family side. Vastu ✔
  • External Staircase (NW boundary) — tenant-only access route, entirely outside main building envelope. Lands on the Tenant West Balcony on FF, then continues as a second flight straight up to the building's north-west corner, arriving on the roof terrace through an open gap in the parapet there — no door, no lock, freely accessible to tenants. No crossover with family circulation.
  • Sit-out: WEST side — open covered porch adjacent to main hall. 10'0"×4'0" as labeled. NOT on East or between bedrooms.
  • Main entrance East — car porch cantilever. FF glass balcony slab cantilevered from West boundary.
  • Plumbing stack: GF toilets T1+T2 cluster North — aligns with FF tenant washrooms above.
  • Central load-bearing columns at corners + mid-span; FF partition walls confirmed non-load-bearing per engineer scope.

FF addition: ~1,060 sq ft × 150 kg/m² additional load. Within existing foundation tolerance per Consulting Engineer. All dimensions to be verified on site before execution.

Privacy & Separation Strategy
Column-based zone isolation
● CENTRAL WALL ISOLATION
A single 9" load-bearing wall runs the full N-S depth, separating the West Column (Hall/Kitchen/Family Rooms) from the East Column (Tenant Rooms/Family Hall). Each Tenant Room has its own door directly across this wall into the Hall or Open Kitchen — no shared corridor on either side.
● TENANT ROOM 1 / ROOM 2 SEPARATION
4.5" partition wall divides Tenant Room 1 (north) and Tenant Room 2 (south). Each has an independent door into the West Column (Hall and Open Kitchen respectively) — no direct Room-1-to-Room-2 interior path.
● TENANT WEST BALCONY
Tenant West Balcony at external staircase landing level. Accessible from the Hall and the external staircase, which continues up from here as a second flight to the building's north-west corner, then through an open (unlocked) gap in the parapet onto the roof terrace. Sized ~4'8"×19'1" (~90 sqft). West-facing, upper balcony. No family access. Stacked directly above the Family West Balcony.
04ENGINEER BRIEF — FIRST FLOOR PROPOSED
SITE: Residence of Mr. R.K. Naveen Kumar, Sagar Court, Mangalore. First floor new construction over renovated GF. East-facing main entrance. Consulting Engineer: Mr. Akshar N Kumar, Konchady, Mangalore. All dimensions approximate — verify on site before execution.

ZONE NOTE (v13 — SKETCH LAYOUT): First floor is re-partitioned around a single central N–S load-bearing wall, splitting the floor into a WEST COLUMN and an EAST COLUMN (instead of the earlier N/S Tenant-over-Family split). WEST COLUMN, top to bottom: Hall (with Toilet in its NE corner) → Open Kitchen (no door, open to Hall) → Family Room 1 → Family Room 2. EAST COLUMN, top to bottom: Tenant Room 1 → Tenant Room 2 → Family Hall (with Family Toilet and the Internal Staircase stacked in its NE corner). The Tenant zone is now exactly 2 bedrooms with no separate Tenant Hall/Kitchen/Toilet of their own — each tenant room opens via its own door directly across the central wall into the Hall or Open Kitchen respectively. All three balconies (Tenant West, Family West, Family Glass South/SE) and the external tenant staircase (NW, outside the building envelope) are unchanged from the prior layout.

KEY POSITION NOTE: Sit-out is on the WEST side of Ground Floor (open porch, 10'0"×4'0"), directly in front of the main entry door. On FF, the WEST COLUMN (Hall, Open Kitchen, Family Room 1, Family Room 2) runs the full N–S depth of the building along the west boundary; the EAST COLUMN (Tenant Room 1, Tenant Room 2, Family Hall) runs the full N–S depth along the east boundary. Main entrance, sit-out, exterior envelope, and all Ground Floor content are unchanged.

06 MATERIALS & DETAILING

Four material decisions carry most of the character of this residence — chosen for climate, light, and longevity, not just appearance.

Frameless Glass
South Balcony · 270 sq ft
Toughened glass balustrade with polycarbonate roof — maximises the enlarged South-facing family balcony's sunset view while staying structurally minimal.
Terracotta Tile
Sit-out Porch · Diamond Lay
Decorative warm-tone flooring across the West sit-out, paired with round columns and pendant lighting — the first surface a guest steps onto.
Structural Concrete
Foundation · ~150 kg/m² FF Load
Existing GF foundation verified within tolerance for the new First Floor addition — load-bearing walls confirmed, partitions kept non-structural.
Mild-Steel Helical Stair
SE Quadrant · Family-Only Ascent
Clockwise helical ascent lands directly into the Family Suite on FF, then continues to roof level through a stair-head enclosure with a hinged, lockable door — compact footprint, fully separated from the tenant's external staircase.
